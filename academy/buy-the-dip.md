Your [DCA bot](/academy/dca-bot) is running.

But what if you want to catch the best price or maybe even the market bottom?

The **Pro** plan offers several options:

## Price limit

**Buy below a certain price level**. No explanation needed.

You can also set multiple bots, buying different amounts at different levels.

## Price drop

**Buy when the price drops a certain percentage** either from the all-time high or in the last 24 hours (to capture moments of market volatility).

## RSI

**Buy when the RSI indicator is below [X]**. This is a classic indicator used by many investors to determine a good entry point. You have two options: buy only when the price is below, or use it as a trigger to start the bot, and it will continue from then on.

Remember that RSI is calculated based on the last "close", not a live value.

## Moving averages

**Buy when a moving average is below [X]**. This is another popular method for evaluating prices, with the daily SMA 200 being a common choice. Whether you use a simple (SMA) or exponential (EMA) moving average, when the price falls below it, you know it's relatively low.

## Spending limit

Finally, you can **set a limit for how much the bot will invest**.

All those options work for any [DCA bot](/academy/dca-bot), but using spending limit you can also easily set a bot that makes a lump sum purchase.
